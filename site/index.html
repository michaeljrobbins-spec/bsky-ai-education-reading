AI in education on Bluesky

An explorer for Bluesky posts about AI detectors, students cheating with AI, and how teachers respond.

This page shows what was written. It does not draw conclusions. Read how this was made before interpreting anything.

How to use this page: choose filters in the row below, or click a bar or row in any chart. Every chart then updates. Throughout the page, gray means all the posts in the sample and blue means the posts that match your current filters.

Whole sample Matching filters

How to read this: each row is a theme, and longer bars mean more posts. A post is put in a theme if it uses certain keywords, listed under each theme name. One post can mention several themes, so the rows add up to more than the total number of posts.

Tone of voice

Click a bar to show only posts with that tone.

Whole sample Negative Neutral Positive

How to read this: a program gave each post a tone score from −1 (very negative wording) to +1 (very positive wording), based on the words it uses. Taller bars mean more posts with that score. The score describes tone of voice, not opinion. A post that supports a policy can still score negative if it uses words like “cheating” or “problem”, and the program does not understand sarcasm.

Word groups

Click a group to show only its posts. Change the number of groups with the “Word groups” filter.

Whole sample Matching filters

How to read this: a program sorted the posts into groups by which words tend to appear together, and each row lists the most common words in one group. Longer bars mean more posts. The groups are numbered rather than named because the program doesn't know what they mean; reading the words and the excerpts is how you decide. Asking for a different number of groups reshuffles them.
